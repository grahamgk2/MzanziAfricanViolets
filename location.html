African Violets in the Western Cape

We are based in Pringle Bay, Western Cape, South Africa, where we grow and hybridise African violets and streptocarpus.

Cape Town and the Western Cape

Customers cannot collect plants from us, so all orders are sent by courier. Local delivery in the Overberg may be possible by special request, so please contact us to ask.

Across South Africa

We send plants and leaves anywhere in South Africa with The Courier Guy. Leaves are packed two at a time in a labelled, sealable plastic packet and placed in the courier box. Plants and leaves are protected with bubble wrap or other packaging where needed. Orders are packed over the weekend and sent on Monday mornings.

Visiting

Our growing area is not open to the public. We do not publish a street address.
