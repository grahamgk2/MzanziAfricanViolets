Propagating African Violets from Leaves

One healthy leaf can grow into a whole new plant. This page points you to three videos by other African violet growers that take you from the leaf, to the babies, to keeping your collection safe. Watch them in this order.

These videos are made by other growers and are not affiliated with Mzanzi African Violets. Methods and conditions differ, so treat them as guides. Each link opens YouTube in a new tab.

1. Start here: the leaf propagation tutorial

African Violets – LEAF PROPAGATION Tutorial (Gray Turtle)

A tutorial on growing new African violets from leaf cuttings. Begin with this one if you have just received your leaves from us, or want to try propagating a leaf from a plant you already own.

2. Once the babies appear: potting up

Potting up Babies from Leaf Cuttings! (Everything Violets)

A leaf cutting takes time to grow plantlets, and then they need to be moved into their own pots. This video covers the next stage: potting up the babies that have grown from your leaf cuttings.

3. Protect your collection: bringing new plants home

African Violets – Bringing New Plants Home – 5 EASY STEPS (Gray Turtle)

This is the quarantine video. New plants, and leaves from other growers, can bring pests such as mites into a collection, so it is wise to keep newcomers apart from your other plants at first. This video takes you through five steps for bringing new plants home safely. See also the pest advice in our care guide.
