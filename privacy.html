Privacy

Mzanzi African Violets is a small hobby business run by Graham Goode in Pringle Bay, Western Cape. This page explains what personal information we collect and how we use it, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • When you send an order: your name, email address, phone number, delivery town, whether delivery is to a door address or a courier locker, an optional street address or locker name, optional notes, and the leaves you ordered.
  • When you send a message: your name, email address and message.
  • If you contact us by email, WhatsApp or Facebook: whatever you choose to tell us, and your contact details on that service.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to you, prepare your invoice, pack your order and arrange courier delivery. We do not use it for marketing, we do not send newsletters, and we do not sell or share it for any other purpose.

Who sees it

  • Web3Forms. The order and contact forms on this site send your details to us by email through the Web3Forms service. They pass through that service on the way to our inbox.
  • The Courier Guy. To deliver your order we give the courier your name, phone number and delivery address or locker.
  • Our email and messaging providers (Gmail, WhatsApp, Facebook), if you contact us there.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

What stays on your device

While you choose leaves, your selection is kept in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you move between pages. It holds only the leaves you picked, not your personal details, and it expires after 30 days or when you cancel the order. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The leaf propagation videos are loaded from YouTube only if you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as needed to complete your order and for our own bookkeeping, then delete them. Messages that are not orders are deleted once answered.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Email mzanziafricanviolets@gmail.com and we will deal with it promptly. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
