How ordering works

Prices

  • Set of two leaves: R30 (one set per cultivar; minimum order is 5 sets, R150)
  • Young miniature or semiminiature plant: R50 (when available)
  • Adult miniature or semiminiature plant: R70 (when available)
  • Standard-sized plants: prices vary, so please ask.

Postage is charged in addition to the prices above.

Payment

Send us your order by email, Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp. We will reply with an invoice (PDF) listing the plants and leaves that are available from your order, the postage quote, the total and our banking details. Payment is by EFT.

Postage and timing

We post across South Africa with The Courier Guy. Orders are packed over the weekend and sent out on Monday mornings.
