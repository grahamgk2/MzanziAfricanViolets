African Violet Leaves and Plants

We mostly sell leaves for propagation. We have only a very limited stock of baby plants and even fewer adult plants, so please contact us to ask what plants are available.

Order leaves

  • African violet cultivars – hybrids and named varieties, with photos.
  • Species African violets – species Streptocarpus (Saintpaulia), with photos and growing notes.

Streptocarpus and other gesneriad lists are coming soon.

How leaves are sold

Leaves are sold in sets of two for R30 per set, limited to one set per cultivar, with a minimum order of 5 sets (R150), plus postage. They are sent by courier. Healthy leaf cuttings can be rooted at home to grow new plants. See the care guide for how.

Plants

Young miniature and semiminiature plants are R50 and adult plants R70 when in stock. Standard-sized plants vary in price. See Orders & Contact.
